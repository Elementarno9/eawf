## Anti-drift verdict

The build HONORS the deterministic-first / jury-second doctrine; no wave thinned its criterion into an idle stub where a real gate was demanded. The auditor refuted each red flag:

- The discrimination gate (W08) is deterministic and its rubber-stamp negative control actually FAILS the gate — not a jury certifying a jury.
- Band enforce is truly band-conditional (enforce False for a non-UI wave; the idle-contract gate makes this a checked CI invariant).
- The W28 security check is a real allowlist mirror of `set_wave_value` — a locked key (empty `writable_layers`) is now refused at the daemon, the modal, and behind a confirm.
- The PoC is grounded in a real in-process `run_action` transcript with an independent cross-check, not hand-waved.
- The live band jury is intentionally idle (honest contract: the dead rung is wired + gated, the quality profile is opt-in, and the canned-ballot + idle-contract gates are the load-bearing always-run CI guards).

## Net-new security finding (delivered)

W28 closed a real gap: `set_layer_value` gated leaf-key existence only and skipped the `writable_layers` check that `set_wave_value` enforces, so a locked key was persistable through the layer RPC. The fix mirrors the allowlist + filters locked keys from the modal + gates `security.*` edits behind a confirm.
