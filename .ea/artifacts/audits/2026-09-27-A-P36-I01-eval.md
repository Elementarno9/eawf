# P36-I01 evaluation audit

## Summary

Iter P36-I01 cut this repository over to epoch 2 and made 0.7.0.dev5 release-ready as the product-canary rung, under decisions D71 to D73 [1]. Ten waves landed before the cut on epoch-1 verbs (W01, W06 to W14), the live cutover ran as W02, and three waves ran after it through the new legacy continuation route (W03 to W05), which runs each Task's decisive gates itself before it completes [2].

The first fresh-context audit returned **pass-with-followups**: all 41 declared criteria across W01 to W14 were met with every gate exiting 0, but five satisfied dispositions cited phase P34 for commits built in P33, and the iter-close full suite found five regressions. The close-out Task EAWF-0001 fixed both in the close-out commit, and a second fresh-context audit of that commit returned **pass** [3].

## What the audit found

**The cutover is exact and reproducible.** The live manifest digest equals the pinned-clone digest at the frozen revision the cutover record names, a second apply selected nothing new, the rollback marker is written, and the frozen epoch-1 `state.json` is unchanged since that revision [4].

**The native roadmap owns every requirement.** The rc1, rc2 and stable plan revisions were applied with operator approval, and the census reads 1,170 ids: 1,108 owned, 24 deferred, 38 satisfied and 0 unowned [5].

**The post-cut commit path works.** After the cut every P36 Task was complete and the commit lint knew only imported keys, so no fix could land. The close-out added a `Task:` trailer naming a claimed or running native Task, and the close-out commit passed its own lint [6].

**The console reads this repository.** The live smoke serves this repository's projection at three widths with no prototype literal, after the projection learned to read imported rows and the launcher learned to resolve authority at the `.ea` tree [7].
