## The keystone: the spec-body authoring + deterministic-gate pipeline is live

The load-bearing outcome is real end-to-end, not idle: a wave's typed `gates` now have an on-disk home (`Wave.gates: list[GateSpec]`, schema 1.8 migration), `_load_gate_specs` reads them (no longer a `return []` stub), a `.ea/specs/<wave>.md` body parses through `WaveSpecBody` + the `_extract_gate_specs`/`_extract_criterion_specs` extractors, `eawf spec sync` runs the EAWF021/EAWF022 lints and materializes the parsed criteria + gates onto the wave's state row via the daemon, and a passing deterministic gate at the close gate mints a `deterministic`/`pass` `EvidenceRecord` that the trust scorecard reads as the `verified` tier. This is the machinery the in-band I13 waves require to close on a deterministic gate rather than the (idle) jury.
