# P31 closeout audit

## Summary

Phase P31 delivered the 0.7.0.dev1 stabilization floor across fifty waves in one iter. Every wave is closed with a pinned commit that is an ancestor of the phase head. The suite stands at 16,070 passing with a single local-only failure that skips in continuous integration. The strict prose gate, the snapshot pairing gate, mypy and the dependency check are all clean.

This phase was executed by harness-dispatched worktree agents rather than by the daemon's own dispatch engine, so no wave carries a captured runtime and every close records an explained runtime waiver. Verification was therefore held outside the close gate: each wave's declared gates were re-run on the integrated branch head before its close, and a fresh-context auditor independently re-ran a sample plus every unique gate argv in the phase.

## What did not land

Two criteria closed unmet, both disclosed in their wave outcomes rather than waived.

**W26 CR-01** required the 0.7.0.dev1 checkpoint to reach APPROVED after a preflight where all eight dev1 gates pass. It did not. Three required readiness signals cannot be satisfied before the merge: ancestry passes only once the head is an ancestor of the publishing branch, the artifact and dependency signals read receipts only the release workflow produces, and the credentials signal has no producer anywhere in the phase. **No Release record was persisted**, so the checkpoint named in the wave title was not cut. The wave did land the version bump and the changelog section, which moved two required signals from failing to passing.

**W25 CR-02** required the full suite to exit zero. It does not, for the single environmental reason above.

Both gaps are scheduled: P32-W23 adds the credentials producer, and P32-W24 cuts the checkpoint to APPROVED on merged main, with P32-W01 now depending on W24. Before this phase, P32-W01 had no dependencies and opened by moving a Release record from APPROVED to PUBLISHING that did not exist.

## What the audit corrected

An independent fresh-context audit contradicted this session's own account on several points, and each correction was verified before acceptance. Eight of the ten failures standing at audit time were introduced by this phase rather than inherited: a workflow reflow that outran the literal its test greps, a module split that moved a symbol without updating its test, and a renderer change whose goldens were never refreshed. The idle-surface ceiling had also been breached by this phase rather than drifting into it. All were repaired.

The audit further found that fifty of the phase's recorded gate arguments no longer resolve, because a late wave collapsed the test tree beneath waves that had already closed. Every one passes once aimed at its new home, so the work is verified; the record is not re-runnable as written. No wave-level reopen verb exists to repair them in place, so a translation map was written instead and the repair is scheduled as P32-W25.

## Verification integrity

The store cannot reconstruct this phase's verification history. Nineteen close-attempt rows exist for fifty closed waves, and the five affordance-parity gates that were re-run persisted no receipt. Two gates are required and blocking yet cannot fail on any tree. Both facts are scheduled as P32-W26 rather than left implicit.
