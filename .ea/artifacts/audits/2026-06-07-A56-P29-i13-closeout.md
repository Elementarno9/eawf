- W12 persisted-evidence gap (process): W12's required criterion was gate-verified live but closed via the `EAWF_DAEMONLESS` in-process carve-out, so it carries no persisted `OracleResult` evidence row. Root cause is a real daemon-robustness scaling concern: the long-running daemon crashed under W12's ~352s affordance-matrix sweep gate (and again under a band-wave floor-pack full-pytest run).
