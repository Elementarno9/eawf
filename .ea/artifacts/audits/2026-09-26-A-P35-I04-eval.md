# P35-I04 evaluation audit

## Summary

Iter P35-I04 is titled "Cut this repository over to epoch 2 and author the rc1 roadmap". It shipped twelve waves (`b6b8aebb` to `c77f8464`, base `c3b5107a`): epoch-1 row converters, a declared default Track, stale-row reconcile, a registered-workspace guard at apply, a `migrate epoch2 --stage-to` verb, the authority epoch in status and doctor, the epoch-1 write fence on a marked root, the backup-verified opt-in canary with a sealed activation, fixes to the P34 epoch-2 audit gaps, a clone rehearsal of this repository's cutover, console verb bindings, and console read fixes. Decision `D69` (which supersedes `D67`) moved the live cutover, the live console smoke, the rc1 roadmap and dev4 release readiness (W13 to W16) to P36-I01-W02 to W05, behind a new prerequisite P36-I01-W01.

This fresh-context audit returns **major**. Every wave gate is green at `599d8181` (518 passed over eleven gate files), and two source mutations each turned their gate red. Two findings are high. W11's console launcher never passes an operator principal, so every bound console write is refused in the live console. W06's new doctor row pushes `git_state_drift` off the rendered viewport, so `tests/tui/test_modes_doctor.py` fails at head. It passes at the iter base, and the main CI pytest lane collects it. A third CI red comes from the requirement trace: `D69` moved three owners, and nobody re-ran `requirement_trace.py write`. The iter and phase titles still promise a cutover and a dev4 release that P35 no longer delivers.

## What the audit found

**The importer and apply guards hold at their gates.** W01's plan test pins zero `no_converter` rows and keeps D-id URNs. W02 pins an assignment count of 39 without the declared Track and 0 with it (`tests/integration/kernel/migration/test_epoch2_plan_mode.py:583`, `:594`). W03's `reconcile_stale_rows` is called from `eawf worktree reconcile` (`src/eawf/surfaces/cli/commands/worktree.py:581`). W04 raises `MigrationWorkspaceNotRegisteredError` at `src/eawf/kernel/migration/epoch2/apply.py:188`. It resolves the key in the machine registry by default, and `--registry-path` overrides that. Both `docs/help/migration.md:34` and `docs/reference/epoch2-cutover.md:20` document the default. W05's `stage_committed_corpus` is called from the CLI (`src/eawf/surfaces/cli/commands/migrate.py:731`), and a malformed key exits 2 with no traceback.

**The fence and the canary seal are wired, with two soft edges.** W07's `refuse_legacy_write` runs in `write_state_unlocked` (`src/eawf/kernel/state/io.py:191`), in the generic writer (`src/eawf/kernel/state/writer.py:43`) and in the CLI mutation path (`src/eawf/surfaces/cli/_mutation.py:137`). The boot sweep checks `epoch_marker_present` (`src/eawf/runtime/daemon/main.py:549`, `:566`). The fence mutation reds 5 of 11 tests. The state row now says exit 2, but its `intent.desired_outcome` and the local spec still say exit 4. W08's `verify_opt_in_backup` gates the apply (`src/eawf/kernel/migration/epoch2/apply.py:606`), and the daemon session calls `seal_first_native_mutation` (`src/eawf/runtime/daemon/epoch2_root.py:346`). A seal failure is logged and retried next session (`src/eawf/runtime/daemon/epoch2_root.py:332`). The canary reversible window reports `open` for every opt-in tree, because nothing closes it (`src/eawf/kernel/migration/epoch2/activation.py:225`). W09's torn-tail repair runs on the real boot path (`src/eawf/runtime/daemon/main.py:1046`), and removing the call reds 5 of 13 tests.

**The rehearsal ran, and three of its defects stay open.** W10's clone rehearsal is applicable and idempotent, and its evidence is under `.ea/artifacts/evidence/2026-09-dev4-cutover-rehearsal/`. The wave fixed D-01, the released-lease quiescence refusal. D-02, D-03 and D-04 are filed as `B188` (P1), which says they must land before the P36 live cut. D-02 matters most: the apply builds from the staged HEAD corpus and never compares it with the working-tree `state.json`. The evidence document still names the live cut "W13", which is now P36-I01-W02.

**The console verbs are bound in tests only.** W11's `ConsoleApp` sends verbs through `ProjectionSeam.request` (`src/eawf/surfaces/tui/console/app.py:595`). That method refuses when the seam holds no operator (`src/eawf/surfaces/tui/console/seam.py:531`). The only production constructor, `_launch_native`, passes none (`src/eawf/surfaces/tui/launch.py:206`). The gate's tests inject an `Operator`, so CR-01 holds in the harness and never in the shipped console. The operator's claim that the attention register has no epoch-2 producer is refuted: the Attention route projects `PENDING_ACTION` rows (`src/eawf/kernel/projection/compute.py:114`), and the delivery approval mutator writes them (`src/eawf/runtime/daemon/methods/delivery_approval.py:487`). The shape of the reconnect claim holds. Run-control request and acknowledge publish no event (`src/eawf/runtime/daemon/methods/run.py:953`, `:964`). Patch events need an `entity_ref` (`src/eawf/kernel/projection/compute.py:422`). So reconnect reconciles by re-sending outstanding operations (`src/eawf/surfaces/tui/console/seam.py:577`), not by reading a patch. W12 holds at its gate.

**The deferral is recorded, and the titles did not follow it.** The body of commit `cd973597` enumerates all four removals and the added P36-I01-W01. `D67` is `superseded` with `superseded_by: D69`. P36-I01 carries W01 to W05, and W02 depends on W01 and on P35-I04-W10. P35-I04 still has the title "Cut this repository over to epoch 2 and author the rc1 roadmap". The P35 phase title ("... and cut over") and its description ("cuts 0.7.0.dev4 ... live epoch-2 cutover (D62) ... then authoring the full P36-P38 plan") also overstate the work. P36-I01's title ("Refuse epoch-1 state, rehearse migration, retire the epoch-1 TUI") does not describe its new waves. Dev4 release readiness now sits in P36-I01-W05, so no P35 wave owns the release that the phase-equals-release rule requires at P35 close. `.ea/requirements.json` still names `P35-I04-W13`, `W15` and `W16` as owners of `REL-011`, `REL-031` and `REL-032`. `REL-031` ("This repository is the dev4 opted-in canary") is left with only W08 in P35.

**Gate-runner `EA_STATE` isolation holds.** With `EA_STATE` exported at a scratch copy of the state, the migration, daemon, console and surfaces trees gave 4,924 passed, 10 skipped. No CLI test still resolves through an inherited `EA_STATE`.
