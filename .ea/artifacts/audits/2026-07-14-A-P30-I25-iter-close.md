# A-P30-I25 — iter-close evaluation: headless dispatch lifecycle consistency

## Summary

Iter P30-I25 set out to make the autopilot lifecycle behave identically for codex and headless-claude, and grew into the repo's deepest measurement repair: 53 waves across a lifecycle cluster (W01-W08), a watch/observability cluster (W09-W24), and a runtime-measurement chain (W25-W53) that took eight audit cycles to converge. A fresh-context auditor re-read the waves against their typed criteria over the `fcc1c51e..HEAD` range [1].

Verdict: **minor** (pass-with-followups). The lifecycle and measurement deliverables hold up against the shipped code — the auditor's falsifier probes drove the production aggregator, not a mock, and confirmed that a 101.75-hour interrupted turn now contributes zero, that the transcript span is never substituted for a measurement, and that the zero-runtime gate resolves enforcement from the fleet verify block rather than the UI band [2]. One real idle contract was found and repaired inside the iter (W53); three residual gaps are recorded as backlog rows rather than blocking the close.

## Evidence

- **Idle contract found and closed (W53).** `ActualSummary.calibration_excluded` shipped in W47 with a writer on the close path and no reader anywhere: `_bucket_actuals` filtered on CLOSED, bucketed, in-window and positive elapsed EU, and never checked the flag. Six I25 actuals carried the flag AND cleared every one of those filters, so a shared-session split figure was re-fitting the effort buckets it was explicitly marked unfit for. W53 makes the exclusion bite in `calibrate_buckets`, `compute_eu_variance`, and `compute_estimate_actual_variance`, leaves `compute_weekly_burn` counting the row (burn measures spend, not estimate quality), and guards each behaviour with a test that reddens when its filter is removed [3]. This was the exact failure class the phase exists to eliminate, caught by the phase's own close audit.
- **Runtime-measurement chain verified against production code.** The auditor deep-verified 23 waves (~50 criteria) by driving `aggregate_transcript_counters` directly: an interrupted turn yields `duration_ms=0`; a giant turn with no interrupt marker yields 0 rather than a span substitution; a `durationMs` on a non-`turn_duration` row is not counted; a single-timestamp resumed file yields 0. `MEASURE_VERSION` is 7 [2].
- **Zero-runtime gate confirmed live, not by test alone.** `load_active_verify_block` resolves `enforce=True` for this repo while the pre-W52 band-narrowed `resolve_wave_verify_block` resolved `enforce=False` for the very waves that closed on it — so the old gate genuinely could not bite. The shipped gate skips the narrowing [2].
- **Accepted-residual rows exist and say what the closes rely on.** `D-RESET-PARDON-STRICT` (keep the pardon strict, pay for it with a visible waiver), `D-I25-EU-EXCLUDE` (names the wall-clock-inflated and shared-session rows), `D-W43-CRITERIA-SUPERSEDED`, and `B111` (interrupt-marker contract regression risk) are all present. W50-W53 closed on the reset-pardon path with `elapsed_eu=0.0` and `calibration_excluded=true` — an honest record of a re-originated baseline, not a silent capture failure.
- **Full-tree suite green.** `just test` on the branch: 13303 passed, 49 skipped, 1 xfailed. The single failure under `-n auto` (`tests/tui/test_zoom_regression.py::test_zoom_focuses_quadrant_roadmap_so_enter_lands`) passes serially and is the known parallel-contention flake.
